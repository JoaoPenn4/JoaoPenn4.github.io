Who runs this tool

This tool is run by João Pedro Penna Sales, an individual in Brazil, as an AliExpress affiliate publisher. Contact details are at the end of this page.

Data we collect

The tool does not ask for names, emails, addresses, payment details or AliExpress accounts. It does not use cookies or analytics.

To answer a request, the server receives the search terms, product IDs and filters that your AI assistant sends. These are used only to query the AliExpress Affiliate API and are not stored by the tool. The server keeps basic technical logs (time of the request and the request path) for troubleshooting.

Data we use from AliExpress

The tool receives public product, price, category and promotion data from the AliExpress Affiliate API and passes it back to your AI assistant. The category list is cached in memory for up to 24 hours. Nothing else is kept, and no AliExpress customer data is ever accessed.

Service providers

  • Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (Brazil) hosts the server.
  • Tailscale provides the secure public address that connects your assistant to the server.
  • Your AI assistant provider (for example Anthropic, for Claude, in the United States) receives the product results so it can show them to you. Their own privacy policy applies to your conversations with the assistant.
  • AliExpress receives the API requests and handles any purchase you make under its own terms and privacy policy.

Data is never sold.

Affiliate links

Product links returned by the tool are AliExpress affiliate links. If you buy through one, AliExpress may pay a commission to the tool's owner at no extra cost to you. AliExpress tracks affiliate clicks and purchases on its own site under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Under Brazil's LGPD you can ask what data about you is held, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Because the tool does not store personal data, there is normally nothing to delete, but you can still get in touch with any question.
