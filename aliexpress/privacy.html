Home Privacy policy

Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect or store names, emails, addresses, payment details or any other personal data. It does not use cookies or analytics.

Data we use

The app sends search terms, product IDs and category IDs to the AliExpress Affiliate API and receives public product, price and promotion data. Search terms are used only to answer the request and are not kept.

Affiliate links

Product links returned by the app are AliExpress affiliate links. If you buy through one, AliExpress may pay a commission to the app owner at no extra cost to you. Purchases, accounts and payments are handled entirely by AliExpress under its own terms and privacy policy.

Third parties

Data is not sold or shared with anyone other than the AliExpress Affiliate API calls described above.
