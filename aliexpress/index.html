Seven tools on the AliExpress Affiliate API

Every call is fixed to shipping to Brazil, prices in reais and Portuguese product text, so Claude never shows an item that cannot reach you.

  • buscar_produtos

    aliexpress.affiliate.product.query

    Search by words, price and category
  • detalhes_produto

    aliexpress.affiliate.productdetail.get

    Details from IDs or links
  • gerar_link_afiliado

    aliexpress.affiliate.link.generate

    An affiliate link to buy
  • listar_categorias

    aliexpress.affiliate.category.get

    Categories, kept for 24 hours
  • produtos_em_alta

    aliexpress.affiliate.hotproduct.query

    Products selling well now
  • listar_promocoes

    aliexpress.affiliate.featuredpromo.get

    Current AliExpress promotions
  • produtos_da_promocao

    aliexpress.affiliate.featuredpromo.products.get

    Products in one promotion

How a search runs

Add the connector to Claude once. Every product question then goes through it to AliExpress and back.

Prices leave out import tax under Remessa Conforme and any coupons, so check the total at checkout.

Run by João Pedro Penna Sales, an AliExpress affiliate in Brazil. Anything else, ask on GitHub.

Ask on GitHub

Does the connector cost anything?

No. If you buy through one of its links, AliExpress may pay me a commission. Your price stays the same.

Are the prices in reais final?

No. They come straight from AliExpress and leave out Remessa Conforme import tax and coupons.

Does it see my AliExpress account?

No. It reads the public affiliate catalogue and never asks for an account, address or card.

How do I get the connector address?

Ask on GitHub. You get the connector address to paste into Claude's connector settings.
