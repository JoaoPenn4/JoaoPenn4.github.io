Affiliate disclosure

Product links returned by this tool are AliExpress affiliate links. If you buy something through one of them, I may earn a commission from AliExpress. You pay the same price, and the commission is how this free tool is kept running.

What it does

A free tool for shoppers in Brazil. You connect it to an AI assistant and ask for products in plain language, for example "a mechanical keyboard under R$ 200". The assistant searches AliExpress for you and shows:

  • products that ship to Brazil, with prices in BRL
  • product details, ratings and sales volume
  • trending products and current AliExpress promotions
  • a link to each product on AliExpress, where you complete any purchase

Prices come straight from AliExpress and do not include import taxes (Remessa Conforme) or coupons.

How to use it

The tool is an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server, which works as a connector for AI assistants such as Claude. Add the connector address in your assistant's connector settings and start asking for products. Access is free; to get the connector address, get in touch through github.com/JoaoPenn4.

AliExpress Affiliate API methods used

  • aliexpress.affiliate.product.query: product search
  • aliexpress.affiliate.productdetail.get: product details
  • aliexpress.affiliate.hotproduct.query: trending products
  • aliexpress.affiliate.category.get: category list
  • aliexpress.affiliate.featuredpromo.get and featuredpromo.products.get: promotions
  • aliexpress.affiliate.link.generate: affiliate link generation

Every request is fixed to ship to Brazil, BRL currency and Portuguese language.

Data

The tool works only with public product and promotion data. It does not ask for accounts, does not process orders or payments, and does not collect buyer personal data. See the privacy policy for details.
